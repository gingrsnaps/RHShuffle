# Start here — V15

1. Back up the currently running private state.
2. Extract the FastDeploy ZIP into the root of a private repository.
3. Confirm `requirements.txt` and `wager_backend.py` are visible at the repository root.
4. Force rebuild and deploy in DigitalOcean.
5. Verify `/healthz` and `/readyz`.
6. Sign in and test Shuffle Schedule, Shuffle Prizes, EmpireDrop Schedule and EmpireDrop Prizes.
7. Confirm the public Shuffle Top 15, EmpireDrop Top 10 and RedPoints Community Leaderboard.

Both schedule editors display Eastern time. EmpireDrop official race dates come from the selected provider slug; the local display calendar does not change EmpireDrop itself.
