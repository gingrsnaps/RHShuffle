Two private cards each. Five shared cards: flop (three), turn (one), river (one). Best five-card hand wins; equal hands split the pot. The dealer button alternates. The button posts the small blind and acts first before the flop, last afterward.

Check when no bet is owed; otherwise Call, Raise or Fold. A raise amount is the total for this betting round. The minimum raise equals the previous full raise; a smaller increase is allowed only all-in. The minimum opening bet is one big blind. Unmatched chips return to their owner. There is no rake or fixed payout multiplier.

Both seats start with your chosen table stack. Big blind: stack ÷ 50, rounded down, minimum 2. Small blind: half the big blind, rounded down, minimum 1. Minimum table stack: 20 RP. The wallet reserves your stack until the hand ends. Refresh resumes the same hand. At the weekly reset an unfinished hand folds before the new allowance.

RedBot is a published, deterministic computer strategy. It sees only its own cards, the revealed board and public betting state. It is not a human player. The completed receipt verifies the shuffle, every decision and the pot. Full fairness specification →
