A commitment before your wager

  1. The server generates a random 32-byte seed and publishes its SHA-256 hash before your bet. The active seed stays private.
  2. Choose your client seed. After reading the commitment, your browser generates another fresh 16 random bytes for this bet. The receipt records both inputs, the game, settings, wager, season and sequential bet number.
  3. HMAC-SHA-256 generates the outcome from those inputs. Balance, wager, result and receipt save in one transaction.
  4. The one-time server seed is revealed immediately. Your browser checks its earlier commitment and independently reproduces the result and payout before showing “Verified.” A fresh committed seed is used for the next bet.

Keep the commitment you saw before playing. A receipt alone proves its calculation is consistent; it does not independently prove when its commitment was first published. This is an inspectable algorithm, not third-party certification, identity verification, a payout guarantee, or a connection to real-money Shuffle games.

The published rules

Dice: An unbiased integer from 0 to 9,999 is displayed as 0.00–99.99. For a win chance of C percent, Under wins below C × 100; Over wins at or above 10,000 − C × 100. A winning bet returns floor(wager × 99 ÷ C) points, including the wager.

Keno: Select 1–10 numbers from 1–40. Ten different numbers are drawn using a partial Fisher–Yates shuffle. Your match count selects the published multiplier. The table updates before betting whenever picks or risk change.

Plinko: Each of 8, 12 or 16 steps is an unbiased left/right decision. The sum of right steps selects the slot. The animation only illustrates those committed decisions; physics does not decide the result.

Returns: Keno and Plinko use openly specified probability-weighted tables, targeting 99% expected return before rounding. Multipliers round down to four decimal places; payouts round down to whole points. Small wagers can have lower effective returns. The exact table and outcome probabilities are available before each bet.

RedPoints are play-only. There are no purchases, cash-outs or Botrix transfers. Your one shared balance resets to 100,000 at Tuesday 6 PM America/New_York, including daylight saving changes. Duplicate requests cannot settle the same bet twice.

Exact payout table mathematics

Let p = 2 for Low, 4 for Medium, or 7 for High. For Keno with k selected numbers and h matches, use probability P(h) = choose(k,h) × choose(40−k,10−h) ÷ choose(40,10), and weight w(h) = hp.

For Plinko with n rows and landing slot s, use probability P(s) = choose(n,s) ÷ 2n, and weight w(s) = ½ + |2s−n|p.

Calculate the probability-weighted mean M = Σ P(i)w(i). Each multiplier in integer units is floor((99 ÷ 100) × w(i) ÷ M × 10,000). Divide units by 10,000 to display the multiplier. The return is floor(wager × units ÷ 10,000). The server uses exact fractions and the independent browser verifier uses integer ratios.

These custom tables reward rarer outcomes more heavily at higher risk. Whole-point rounding can make small wagers return materially less than the nominal 99% expectation; the payout table shows the exact returned points before each wager.

Exact v1 byte format

Decode the 64-character server-seed hex string into 32 bytes. SHA-256 of those bytes is the commitment. For HMAC, use those bytes as the key. The ASCII message is compact JSON of:

["redpoints-v1", season, game, client_seed, client_salt,
 nonce, wager, options, block_number]

Object keys are sorted, with no spaces around separators. Block numbers start at zero. Read the HMAC digest in four-byte big-endian integers. To choose from N outcomes, reject values at or above 2³² − (2³² mod N), then use value mod N. Consume the next four bytes for the next draw; generate the next HMAC block when necessary.

The configured package includes fairness.py, the independent browser implementation static/fairness.js, and the optional offline verifier tools/verify_redpoints.py. These games have their own published rules; they do not claim compatibility with another operator's receipts or payout tables.
