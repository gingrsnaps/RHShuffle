- Unified Shuffle and EmpireDrop presentation under the fixed title **RedHunllef Wager Race**.

- Added focused task layouts and a consistent save experience.

- Preserved the official EmpireDrop race endpoints and both API-key headers.
- Preserved the five-second privacy-safe Gaming leaderboard updates, ETags and hidden-tab polling pause.
- Added V15 route, template, calendar, Gaming and provider regression tests.
